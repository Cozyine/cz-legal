Discord security bot for Roblox servers

Cozine watches your Discord server for Roblox exploiters and cheaters. When a flagged user joins, moderators get an instant alert in the channel you choose, with the evidence attached. Turn on the kick action and Cozine removes them automatically.

Auto-scan on join

Every new member is checked against the Cozine registry the moment they join.

Alerts or auto-kick

Choose alert-only so mods decide, or let Cozine kick flagged exploiters by itself.

Moderation panel

Look up any Discord user using the /skid command, check their record, and manage your server from the web panel (requirements and TOS applies).

How to add Cozine to your server

  1. Click this link to add Cozine to your Discord server.
  2. Cozine will ask for these permissions: View Channels, Send Messages, Send Messages in Threads, Embed Links, Attach Files, Read Message History, and Manage Webhooks. Manage Webhooks is required for trace_setup and to auto-receive alerts if an exploiter joins your server. If you don't need that, you can disable it.

FAQS

What is Cozine bot?

Cozine is a Discord security bot that detects Roblox exploiters. When a flagged user joins your server, you choose what happens: get an alert for your mods, or let Cozine kick automatically.

Is Cozine free?

Cozine has a free tier with basic scanning and Exclusive access with many more features and modes like:

  • 600+ monitored servers (instead of 150+)
  • 5 second cooldown per skid use (instead of 60)
  • More modes like detailed/ultra detailed
  • Access to messages and roles buttons

How do I get Exclusive Access?

You must meet these requirements:

  1. You must not have any history of being/participating in exploit servers.
  2. You must be a moderator for a large Roblox game or Discord server.
  3. You must agree to and follow our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not install or use the bot.

Note: Meeting these requirements does not guarantee access. Approval is granted at our team's decision.
