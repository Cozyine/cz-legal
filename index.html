All information is collected through public data tracking and platform integration:

Data is used strictly to keep communities safe and identify users in violation of Discord or Roblox Terms of Service. This includes:

We recognize that security researchers, investigators, or innocent users may occasionally be flagged. If you believe your data was logged in error, you can easily file an appeal through our support server. Each case is reviewed manually.

  • Access is strictly limited to bot administrators

What Cozine Does

Cozine monitors Discord servers and flags users who have been documented as violating Discord or Roblox Terms of Service. Specifically, we flag users for:

  • Exploiting game mechanics in Roblox games
  • Using Roblox cheats or hacks

Servers can opt-in to receive automated notifications using the designated setup command. When a flagged user joins a configured server, moderators will receive an alert. We are not responsible for individual server ban decisions. Moderators make the final call on whether to take action.
